Transparency

Privacy Policy

This page explains what data remains on your device and when external services receive information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Library, reading history, settings, downloads, glossaries, translation cache, and API keys are stored locally on your device or in backups you create.

Translation and External Services

When selecting Google Translate, DeepL, Gemini, or OpenRouter, recognized text from chapters is sent to the selected service to generate the translation. Each provider adheres to its own privacy policy. ML Kit can download models and process supported translations entirely on-device.

Yomotsu does not operate a proprietary server to collect your API keys or chapter content.

Sources, Extensions, and Trackers

Extensions directly access sites chosen by the user. Trackers such as AniList, MyAnimeList, and similar services receive data only when explicitly configured. These services are independent of Yomotsu and apply their own terms of service.

Permissions and Backups

Storage access is used exclusively to read and save chapters, translations, and backups. You decide where files are stored and with whom they are shared. Backups may contain your library, reading history, and preferences; handle them as personal data.

Telemetry and Open Source

The Yomotsu project does not operate an advertising network, sell user data, or run behavioral tracking analytics. The entire source code is auditable on GitHub.

Contact and Changes

Questions or suggestions can be submitted through the official GitHub repository. This policy may be updated when new features modify data processing practices.
